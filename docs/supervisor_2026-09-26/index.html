4 · Why F1 is a tie

Each silenced wrong picture is worth as much as one lost right one, and the detector misses most sounds before the gate sees them. With a perfect detector the gate is significant (+0.067).

6 · Pictures — blind human test, 54 sounds on fresh clips
Glance test. Each picture shown 1.5 s at 384 px; the rater types what makes the sound. Old vs new model: 14 → 26 of 54 recognised (+22 points, significant). One new-model picture showed a wrong object (a ringing phone drawn as a desk bell), so the final setup must pass that check too. Three automatic picture checkers failed against the human, so the claim rests on a person.

9 · What an examiner will ask — our answers
  • One annotator. A second person on 30 clips is planned (agreement will be reported).
  • TEST: 5 deliberate reads, 10 exposures counting prints and diagnostics — each dated and disclosed; the final table was scored once under a rule written first.
  • The detector's threshold was picked on a clip set that shares 35 TEST clips — both systems use it; a clean DEV-only check also picked it. Disclosed.
  • The caption baseline had no gate. A fair one (same gate, text instead of pictures) ties with pictures: −0.04 [−0.22, +0.14].
  • Viewer cost was added after F1 came out a tie — its weights date from September; we show the whole cost curve, not one point.

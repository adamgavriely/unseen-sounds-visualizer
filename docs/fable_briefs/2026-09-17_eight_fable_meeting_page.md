FACTS (all verified in the repository):
- Pipeline, 7 stages: 1 FFmpeg audio extraction; 2 OWLv2 object finder (candidates only); 3 Whisper speech-to-text (context only, never drawn); 4 BEATs sound detector (527 AudioSet classes, 2-s windows, bar 0.35); 5 Qwen2.5-VL-7B reasoning: is the source visible? (three differently worded votes per 5-s stretch, silence only if visible in every stretch), what should the picture show (2-5-word phrase + place), one picture per source (AudioSet ontology dedup); 6 FLUX.1-schnell picture on white, side panel <=3 slots, 1.5-s dwell; 7 evaluation: a VLM describes the panel, a judge LLM (Mistral-7B) scores 0-4 against a reference sentence. Baselines: blind (picture for every sound, never looks) and caption (sound name as text).
- Headline (100 test clips, human-corrected reference): gated 3.17 vs blind 3.16 (tie; CI -0.15..+0.18); caption 2.89; perfect gate 3.62, both systems ~0.45 below it in opposite places (ours loses where a picture is due: 2.78 vs 3.02; wins where none is due: 3.56 vs 3.30). Cost: panel on 47% vs 60% of a clip; wasted panel time where nothing was due 357 s vs 506 s (-30%).
- Tried and failed (bars declared before each run): detector — threshold sweep (0.35 already best), CLAP second opinion (recall 50%), PANNs agreement (24/77 phantoms removed, bar 40), persistence+rank rule (same curve), audio-LLM veto (25/77), Demucs separation (made detection worse: 17/23 real lost); visibility — 32B VLM (silences more visible sources 67% vs 35% but wrongly silences 25% of off-screen sounds). Evaluation references — independent four-model reference (55%, chance), with-sound vs muted description (49.5%), gap-closing score (AUROC 0.53): all fail at "is the source in view?".
- Why the detector fails: 7 of 11 withheld pictures on test are sounds under loud speech/music (crowd 0.22 under Speech 0.84; rain 0.23 under Music 0.81); ~80% of unnecessary pictures on dev are phantoms (a whale at a Christmas market at 0.36). One dial: raising the bar removes phantoms and loses real sounds along one curve (figure exists).
- Demos (test clips, Figure 2 of the thesis): A police car on screen -> correct silence (ours 4, blind 3); B New York street, cars visible but the label says off-screen traffic -> wrong silence (ours 0, blind 3; debatable label; debug view shows "0.36 Vehicle - visible"); C ambulance behind the camera -> correct picture (ours 4, blind 3; a second "horn" picture for the Vehicle sound appears because the road left the frame in one stretch). Clean and debug versions of each, 16-18 s.
- Data: 274 clips, 10-20 s, four scenarios (unseen / mixed / seen / no ambient), labelled by Adam alone with the professional captioners' rule (DCMP): "does the picture already tell the viewer this sound is happening?". Sources: keyword-scraped ambient-walk videos (YouTube), AudioSet strongly-labelled segments, UnAV-100 multi-event clips (CC-BY), curated film/TV scenes, Wikimedia Commons / Internet Archive public-domain clips; four sourcing strategies all yielded 15-20% usable clips (proposal said 300; 274 reached). 100 test (25 per scenario), 174 development for settings. Adam's self-agreement 78% (kappa 0.60, intra-rater); no second annotator. External gold: DCASE 2025 Task 3 (human on/off-screen labels): our visibility decision agrees 66.7% +-6; keeps 97% of off-screen sounds, silences 35% of on-screen ones.
- DHH evidence: literature only. HomeSound (CHI 2020) and SoundWatch (ASSETS 2020), Jain et al.: DHH users want awareness of knocks, alarms, sirens, baby crying, dog barking; prefer icons/pictures to text; trust collapses with false alarms. Caption studies (DCMP guidelines; Revuelta et al. 2020 EEG study; Kushalnagar et al.): non-speech sounds are inconsistently captioned or omitted when "visible". NOTHING in this project was tested with DHH viewers: the proposal specified automatic evaluation; the judge is a proxy; a 2-3 hearing-people muted-clip check was designed but not run.

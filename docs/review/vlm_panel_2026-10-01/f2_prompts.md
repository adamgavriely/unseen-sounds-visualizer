VLM-Fable 2 — reliable yes/no + grounding for the gate (read: reason.py _ask/_ab/_sound_is_visible/_scene_fit; Rounds 43–62, 50L; fB_vision.md)

Diagnosis in one line each. Letter a/b = option-ID token prior (PriDe, Zheng+ ICLR'24) — Round 54's 88 % "(a)". "No" habit + 4-token truncation = readout, fixed by the logit read (62: argmax is yes/no on 99.5 %). What is NOT yet fixed: (i) the "none/nothing" escape kills the chain before any crop (50L: 98/145 unnamed, AUROC 0.597 overall vs 0.771 where a crop exists); (ii) the bias twin uses "NOT", and VLMs handle negation poorly (NegBench, CVPR'25: near chance; CLIP-centred, so for Qwen3.8 this is a hypothesis — 62's twin did track s(Q) on 14/14 scene asks); (iii) still frames/crops cannot show "right now": video MLLMs answer from static appearance, not motion (PixFoundation 2.0; AVATAR and AV-ConfuseBench: present ≠ sounding, and MLLMs are visually dominated — a visible object is read as sounding). Rules: no "NOT" in any prompt; no generated verdicts — logit margins only; t fixed on gate-gold (19/32 bar), never at 0, so a yes-ratio shift cannot "pass" (Mirage of CD, 2504.10020: null-image contrast gains on POPE were ratio shifts).

1. NAME-ALL → crop margin (fixes the naming bottleneck; new vs 50L: no escape, k candidates, describe-mined candidates, negation-free twin).
 Prompt N (per stretch, gate's 6 frames, max_new 40): "A {label} sound is heard while these frames are on screen. List the 3 visible things most likely to be making it, most likely first, one short noun phrase per line. Always give 3, even if none fits well." Parse: first 3 non-empty lines, _clean_phrase ≤ 5 words; add noun phrases from the CACHED describe answer (gate_votes 'desc', 0 extra asks); dedupe → ≤ 4 candidates. A forced list on a true off-screen sound is the point: the crop question, not the escape, says no (50L bell_miami 'the church' −5.6/−6.0/−6.3).
 Ground + crop each candidate exactly as Round 50 (BOX re-prompt with the phrase, 20 % margin, short side 224); a stretch where no phrase grounds gets one language-free crop: ViCrop rel-att (the model's own attention under Q+, training-free, 2502.17422). Crop question Q+ (verbatim 50): "This is a close-up cut from a video frame. Is this {phrase} making the {label} sound right now? Answer yes or no." Twin Q− (affirmative, one proposition, replaces the NOT twin): "This is a close-up cut from a video frame. Is this {phrase} just sitting there, with the {label} sound coming from somewhere else? Answer yes or no."
 Readout: m = [s(Q+) − s0(Q+)] − [s(Q−) − s0(Q−)], s = max logit(yes ids) − max logit(no ids) at the first answer token (reason._yes_no_margin), s0 = the same prompt on a grey 224×224 crop, once per label with phrase "this thing" (disclosed approximation of a per-phrase prior; post-hoc debias, 2403.05262: uniform scores on a content-free input). Two aggregations pre-registered, picked on step-0 AUROC: rank-1 candidate only (50L's shape) vs max over candidates — max is an OR that gives every off-screen needed sound 3–4 chances at a spurious "making it"; kept-needed loss under max is the named risk (as_explosion holds 6 of the 33). Sound A = ⌊n/2⌋+1-th largest stretch margin (50L). Rule, two-sided (fB ACTION-MARGIN shape): silenced iff (shipped majority AND A ≥ t_lo) OR A > t_hi — i.e. ADD-seen above t_hi, un-silence below t_lo, so the church crop's −5.6/−6.0/−6.3 rescues bell_miami; REPLACE (silenced iff A > t) reported only.
 Cost/video: per stretch 1 × 40-token + ≤ 4 × 30-token generations + 8 prefill passes ≈ 10–15 s H200; median 6 stretches. Expected: crops on ≥ 120/145 stretches; AUROC ≥ 0.70 (0.771 on cropped sounds today); gate-gold 19–22 / 32–34, with the 15/35 branch now reachable through t_lo.

3. TEMPORAL-CONTRAST ACTION MARGIN (the moment, not presence; new vs fB ACTION-MARGIN: within-video control window, timestamped frames, two-branch rule).
 Frames: HUMAN-2's onset-dense set t0 −1.0, −0.3, −0.1, 0, +0.1, +0.3 s, each preceded by its time as text ("<-0.3 seconds>" … "<0 seconds>", Qwen3-VL text–timestamp alignment, 2511.21631). Q: "Each frame is labelled with its time. A {label} sound starts at the 0 seconds frame. At 0 seconds, is something in view visibly doing the action that makes that sound (striking, swinging, flowing, calling, flashing, running)? Answer yes or no." Control: the same prompt on 6 frames around t_c = the clip time where the family's BEATs + FlexSED frame scores are lowest and ≥ 3 s from any same-family span (label-free, online; none → no control).
 Readout: d_t = s(onset) − s(control) (reference subtraction, as BUZZY/VDD but with a content-matched reference: the same scene without the sound, so church/golfer/street priors cancel) and d_abs = s(onset) − s0. Stretch seen iff d_t > t1 OR d_abs > t2; the second branch is what keeps constant visible sources (storm Rain, waves, aviary Bird: control ≈ onset, d_t ≈ 0 — exactly the HUMAN-2/HUMAN-BOX losses); no control → branch 2 only. ADD-seen on the shipped majority. If step 0 is flat, add Motion-as-Prompt trajectories (CoTracker polylines drawn on the frames, frozen Qwen3-VL: +4–9 pts on motion QA, 0.6–1.9 s/video, 2608.11655).
 Cost: 2 prefill/stretch + one 6-frame control sample. Test: gate-gold AUROC of d_t over the 25 shipped-kept seen vs the 33 shipped-kept needed (STOP < 0.65), t1/t2 by the 19/32 bar (t2 may be pre-set on VGGSounder's visible/audible tags, 2508.08237, or AVATAR's 670 off-screen frames — external, gate-gold untouched), then DEV. Witnesses: golf Whack (needed; golfer standing in both windows → d_t ≈ 0 → kept, lost only if the 0 s frame shows a swing), bell_miami (church in both → d_t ≈ 0, d_abs below t2 → kept).

Not recommended: few-shot image exemplars — multimodal ICL is mostly text-driven and 6-frame exemplars ×k exceed the window (2604.13403); CoT/thinking on every stretch — fluent but ungrounded (CoRGI 2508.00378; 61c 5 min/picture, no gain) → only fB's margin-routed thinking, queued; "answer with one word" is harmless under a logit read and harmful under generation (the 4-token cut); event-vs-object phrasing already at 0.627 (62b).

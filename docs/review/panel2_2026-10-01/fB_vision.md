# Fable B (vision / VLM lens) — what was NOT tried

Fact that frames all three ideas (grep over src/ + benchmark/gold/): token log-probs are read only for Qwen3-Omni (dev_listener, omni_gate N, listener_afnext, gate_audio_veto). The Qwen3.8-27B gate — `_ask`/`_ab`/`_sound_is_visible`, SIGN, SIGN-2, HUMAN/HUMAN-2/HUMAN-BOX, SCENE_FIT (Round 60), 61 Q1/Q3 — has ALWAYS been greedy text + first-letter/first-word parse with a 4–6-token cap. Every "VLM failure" of the day is that readout: letter position (54: "(a)" 88 %), "no" habit (54b: 9/10 (no,no)), truncation (61 step 0: 10/10 "Based on the"; Round 60 shipped two drops that were parser-None). Round 54's own reading: SIGN is "untested rather than refuted". No gate round has read P(yes) vs P(no). One harness, three rules; all online (one video's frames, fixed numbers from gate-gold 41 seen / 38 needed, 145 stretches, 49 DEV judge clips; the 415 has no visibility labels so it cannot select gate rules).

**1. LOGIT-GATE (first pick).** Mechanism: one forward pass per question, score s = max logit(yes ids) − max logit(no ids) at the first answer token (dev_listener convention, lines 180–200), on the gate's own content rewritten as yes/no WORDS, not letters: (a) the shipped a/b content, (b) SIGN-2's effect pair (source-free: "visible sign/effect of {label}"), (c) MAKES_SOUND for the named/described thing. Bias-cancel: d = s(Q) − s(¬Q) (the "no" habit and letter habit are an additive term that cancels; SIGN-2's (no,no) pairs still carry a margin). Verdict per stretch = d > t, t fixed on gate-gold under the standing bar (seen silenced ≥ 19 with needed kept ≥ 32, or ≥ 15 / ≥ 35); clip rule unchanged. Why new: continuous, truncation-free, bias-cancelled readout; the effect question (SIGN) becomes testable for the first time. Targets (D): storm_16200 / storm_7200 Thunder 0.06 (0-vote "nothing" on every stretch), un_driving Explosion 13.52 (Fireworks, "nothing"), london Vehicle 0.25 (2nd stretch "nothing" blocks silencing), tg_d127 Water, tg_d128 Laughter (no cache; DEV arm only); on the 2-1 kills (bell_miami ×3, ambulance 7.3 name=no/ab+desc=yes) d acts as the tie-breaker either way. Expected DEV: 1–3 of 6 visible silenced (−2 each at w=2) ± bell/ambulance (+4 each if flipped to kept): cost 2.028 → ~1.97–1.93; as_explosion (6 needed, all "nothing") is the loss risk, reported by name. Selection: step 0 on gate-gold = AUROC of d (seen vs needed) over 145 stretches with a pre-set STOP bar (e.g. < 0.65 → mechanism dead, 2 and 3 do not run); then t fixed there; then the real test = DEV arm on D's saved specs via the existing sign_screen re-place-and-score harness (gate-gold uses gold labels and has no tg_* cache, so it sees only 3 of D's 6 visible wrongs). Effort: ~2 h (harness reuses sign2_gate + dev_listener's logit read; gate-gold 145 × 3 questions × 2 polarities ≈ 15 GPU-min; DEV arm ≈ 30 min). Risk: Qwen3 template with enable_thinking=False may emit an empty think block — verify where the first answer token lands before any number (print apply_chat_template once). Per-video cost: stretches × questions × 2 polarities prefill passes, no generation (~12–24 passes, seconds on an H200; cheaper than today's 2 × 6-token generations). Trigger: always; no thinking.

**2. ACTION-MARGIN (kill side; Adam's hits-first makes a hit worth 4 vs 2 per visible).** Mechanism: HUMAN-2's step-3 question ("at that moment {candidate} is visibly DOING the thing that makes {label}") on its onset-dense frames (t0 −1.0, −0.3, −0.1, 0, +0.1, +0.3 s), read as d (rule 1), not as yes/no. A sound the shipped majority silences is UN-silenced iff d < a_lo on a majority of its stretches; a_lo fixed on gate-gold so that ≤ 2 of the 16 seen-silenced return. Why new: HUMAN-2 got whack/bell/pet-shop right and aviary/motorcycle wrong as a BINARY; p2's "nothing separates the kills from the 8 silenced-seen with the same vote pattern" used 6 binary signals — a continuous action margin is a signal nobody has had. Targets: bell_miami Bell 0.2, b3_pet_shop Bird 0.1, ambulance Vehicle 7.3 (gate-gold cached: 'church bell' ×3, 'a small bird' ×6, 'nothing'); tg_d133 Fart ×2 only in the DEV arm. Expected DEV: +1–2 hits at +1–2 visible (aviary Bird, marrakech motorcycle are the known re-shows): −0.06 to −0.11 at w=2 if 1:1. Selection: gate-gold (needed kept must rise ≥ 2 with seen silenced ≥ 14) then DEV arm vs D. Effort ~2 h after rule 1's harness; risk: the margin may be flat on still frames (HUMAN-BOX: crops said no on most visible sources). Per-video: 1 open naming (24 tokens) + 2 prefill passes per silenced stretch only; trigger: the shipped gate said "seen"; no thinking.

**3. MARGIN-ROUTED THINKING (Adam: think only when unsure).** Mechanism: a stretch with |d| < m (m fixed on gate-gold as the band where rule 1's verdict is at chance) gets ONE thinking-on ask (Qwen3.8-27B, 2048 budget, the yes/no content, last-line parse + one re-prompt), and that verdict replaces the logit vote on that stretch only; cap K = 2 per video, lowest |d| first. Why new: thinking was only ever run on everything (DCASE swap: 51/78 vs 56/83 off; 61c v-think on all 44, 5 min each, stopped), never routed by the VLM's own uncertainty; 61c's post-hoc "unsure" cut used audio DASM, not a vision margin. Targets: whichever D pictures land in the band (bell 2-1 ×3 and ambulance are the likely ones). Expected DEV: 0–2 pictures; honest prior is weak (thinking not better on average). Selection: gate-gold band accuracy with vs without thinking, then DEV arm. Effort ~2 h (≈ 30 band stretches × 1.5 min). Risk: mid-thought cuts (61c parser weakness). Per-video: ≤ 2 thinking asks ≈ ≤ 3 min; trigger fixed (|d| < m); everything else prefill-only.

\section{Prompts used in the system}\label{app:prompts}
All questions are asked with greedy decoding and, for Qwen3.8-27B, thinking off. $\langle$label$\rangle$ is the family
name, lower-cased and cut before a comma or bracket. Source: \fp{src/stage5_cross_modal_analysis/reason.py},
\fp{benchmark/listener_round.py}, \fp{benchmark/gold/listener_variants.py}, \fp{src/stage6_visual_augmentation/group.py},
\fp{depict.py}, \fp{verify.py}, \fp{picture_templates.py}.

\begin{description}[style=nextline]
\item[Gate: name vote] ``These frames are from the moment a sound of $\langle$label$\rangle$ was heard. Name the thing
  in these frames that is making that sound. Answer with a short noun phrase of at most 5 words. If nothing that could
  make that sound is visible in these frames, answer exactly: nothing.''
\item[Gate: follow-up (text only)] ``Sound heard: $\langle$label$\rangle$ / Thing visible in the video:
  $\langle$named$\rangle$ / Could that thing be what is making that sound? Answer yes or no.''
\item[Gate: a/b vote (asked in both orders)] ``These frames are from the moment a sound of $\langle$label$\rangle$ was
  heard. Judge from the frames alone. (a) you can SEE $\langle$label$\rangle$ happening on screen -- the source is in the
  frames and visibly making that sound (b) $\langle$label$\rangle$ is not visibly happening in these frames. Answer with
  the letter only.''
\item[Gate: describe vote] ``Describe what is happening in these frames in one sentence. Name what you see and what it
  is doing. No more than 25 words.''
\item[Speech context (text only, both orders)] ``\ldots{} Are they (a) reacting to that sound or talking about it, or
  (b) not referring to that sound?''
\item[Scene check (witness rule; logit read)] ``Could the sound of $\langle$label$\rangle$ plausibly be heard in this
  scene? Answer yes or no.'' and the twin ``Could the sound of $\langle$label$\rangle$ NOT plausibly be heard in this
  scene? Answer yes or no.''
\item[Listener yes/no (Qwen3-Omni, Audio Flamingo Next)] ``Is the sound of $\langle$label$\rangle$ present in this recording? Answer
  yes or no.''
\item[Listener inventory] ``List every distinct non-speech sound you hear in this recording, one per line, most prominent
  first.'' (answer limited to 64 tokens, about 50 words)
\item[Grouping (Qwen3-Omni, both orders)] ``You hear a $\langle$label$\rangle$ sound near the start and again near the end
  of this recording. Is the $\langle$label$\rangle$ near the end $\langle o_1\rangle$, or $\langle o_2\rangle$? Answer with
  exactly one word: $\langle w_1\rangle$ or $\langle w_2\rangle$.'' with the options ``the same continuing sound as at the
  start (for example one alarm or engine with a short pause)'' and ``a new, separate event (for example a second bark,
  knock or shot)''.
\item[Depict-event] ``Is "$\langle$event$\rangle$" visibly happening in these frames? Answer yes or no.'' and ``Do these
  frames show no "$\langle$event$\rangle$" happening? \ldots''; then (text only) ``Could the sound of $\langle$B$\rangle$ be
  mistaken for the sound of $\langle$A$\rangle$?'' in both directions.
\item[Picture check] ``Look at this picture. What is the main thing shown in it? Choose exactly one option. \ldots{}
  Answer with the letter only.'' For alarm-type sounds the intended option is the larger thing the picture shows
  (the subject written by the alarm step below), not the device named by the label.
\item[Alarm-family host question (text only)] ``A sound detector heard: $\langle$label$\rangle$. The current picture
  description is: $\langle$subject$\rangle$. Kinds of $\langle$label$\rangle$ in the sound list: $\langle$kinds$\rangle$.
  \ldots{} If it is a PART of a larger thing, answer that whole larger thing in one to three words, something a viewer
  knows by its shape, general enough to cover every kind listed, never one of the kinds themselves, never a person, never
  a place. If it is a whole object on its own, answer MOUNT: and the surface a person usually sees it on \ldots{} Then
  write a semicolon and, in 3 to 8 words, the visible change on that object itself when it goes off.'' The picture
  subject becomes ``a whole $\langle$host$\rangle$ seen from the front with no writing on it, its $\langle$label$\rangle$
  going off, $\langle$sign$\rangle$'' or ``one large $\langle$label$\rangle$ going off, fixed on a $\langle$mount$\rangle$
  \ldots''; full text in \fp{src/stage6_visual_augmentation/host.py}.
\item[Picture prompt tail] ``, the whole thing fully in frame, caught at the moment it makes the sound with its visible
  effect, one large subject filling the picture, plain white background''; negative prompt: scenery, landscape,
  background scene, room interior, street, buildings, sky background, text, letters, watermark.
\end{description}

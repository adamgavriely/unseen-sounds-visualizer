\section{A side-by-side on unseen AudioSet clips}\label{app:fresh}

As a second check, the final system and the two proposal baselines were run on 340 AudioSet-Strong clips that no rule
in this report had seen. They come from a set of 422 clips built for this purpose; the clips are sorted by YouTube
identifier and split into parts, and the last two parts (82 clips) were dropped for time before any score was read.
Nothing was tuned on these clips, and only which sounds are shown and when was scored (no pictures were drawn). The
needed sounds are the 65 AudioSet labels in these clips that belong to the 33 important sound kinds fixed on 28 September;
other labelled sounds count neither way. Repeats of a sound at most 2\,s apart are joined, and hits, wrong pictures and
the cost are counted as in Section~\ref{sec:metric}, with a paired clip bootstrap of 100{,}000 draws
(Table~\ref{tab:fresh}). These tests are outside the Holm family of Section~\ref{sec:support}.

The final system draws less than half as many wrong pictures as direct audio-to-image for a lower cost, and its cost
is close to showing nothing; none of the three differences is significant. AudioSet labels only a few sounds per clip
and do not mark visible sources, so correct pictures of unlabelled sounds count as wrong and on-screen sounds count as
needed; this is a rough side-by-side on unseen clips, not a headline number. The script and results are
\fp{benchmark/gold/fresh_side_by_side.py} and \fp{benchmark/gold/fresh_side_by_side.json}.

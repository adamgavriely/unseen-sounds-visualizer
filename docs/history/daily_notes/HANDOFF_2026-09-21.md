## Tagging rules given to Adam (so the next model answers the same way)

- **visible** = the *source* is on screen while it sounds (a buzzer inside a dashboard = unseen; a door you watch closing = visible). **obvious** = a viewer with no sound would already assume the sound (a walker's steps, steam from a visible kettle, a whistle with a visible puff). **needed** = not visible and not obvious. Obvious decides; visible is only recorded.
- **importance** (rule changed 2026-09-22, Adam + Fable ×2; the old "seen → 1" rule made the mixed class impossible: a dog on camera barking + a siren off screen was "unseen") = **a property of the sound, not of the screen**. = how much you would lose if you did not know this sound happened, rated as if the screen were black; *visible* / *obvious* are separate ticks. 1 = I would not care that I missed it (traffic hum, wind, rain, crowd murmur, background birds, a distant dog bark that adds nothing to the scene); 2 = it helps me understand the scene, I would want a caption (a door, dishes, footsteps that tell me someone is coming, a dog barking near the action, a tool); 3 = I must know it (siren, alarm, explosion, gunshot, glass, baby cry, doorbell, phone, horn, scream, a dog barking at someone). "Can I say what happened?" is only the entry ticket for 2; the sound must also matter to the scene. When unsure between two tiers, pick the lower; for a seen sound unsure between 1 and 2: would a hearing viewer notice it on its own and name its source? yes = 2. Tag from the audio and video only, never from what the pipeline would draw. Confirmed 2026-09-22 by three more reviewers (two rounds; unanimous NUMBER over a binary "clear" tick for seen sounds; same-family collision = hit with the count printed; an FA-weight-2 row rejected as it favours the gated system). Metric: only sounds rated 2–3 are scored; a false alarm counts 1 whatever the sound. Category: mixed = one needed sound rated 2–3 and one visible/obvious sound rated 2–3 (unchanged; a label-based variant was tried and reverted on 2026-09-22 — Adam: the category follows his numbers only). Re-check only: (1) clips that come out "mixed" (filter "I tagged: mixed") — is the visible sound a real event; (2) optional: needed rows rated 2 with a texture label → 1 if you would not care. Needed-sound ratings need no re-check (old and new definition agree for unseen sounds).
- One row for a continuous sound, even if quieter under speech (tick "covered"); split only when it stops for > 2–3 s (e.g. siren drowned by explosions, then back). Do **not** split a sound into seen/unseen halves unless the source is on screen for most of it; a 1-s glimpse → unseen. A same sound heard 0–11 s off screen then seen 11–19 s → two rows (that clip is "mixed").
- Simultaneous destruction noises (building collapse + metal scraping) → one row, family impact. Storm roar → its own row if audible on its own.
- Delete rows with texture/undrawable labels ("Video game sound", "Mechanisms") or wrong human labels (a mouth noise tagged "Caterwaul"). Laughter, cough etc. are now drawn → tag them as needed sounds when off screen.
- Unknown animal calls → label "animal calls (birds/frogs?)", family **Animal** (matches any bird/frog/goose picture in the scorer).
- Slice B: labels and times are human; Adam only ticks visible/obvious/importance/covered, deletes what he doesn't hear, presses done.
- "covered by speech/music" does not change hit/miss or importance; it marks the hard stratum (target ~40).

# Good morning — what happened overnight, in plain words

## 1. "The picture comes too early" — FIXED (on the development clips)

**What was wrong.** Our sound detector usually heard the sound at the right time. Then two later
steps in our own code pulled the start *earlier*, and nothing ever pulled it later. One step even
subtracted a flat 1.5 seconds. So the siren picture appeared 1.5 s before the siren.

**What I changed.** One rule: *a later step may make a start more exact, but may never move it
earlier.* Three reviewers agreed on it before I built it. **It is switched off until you decide**
(`ONSET_MONOTONE` in `config.py`).

**Did it work?** Yes, and clearly:

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| needed sounds shown at the right moment | 11 | **15** |
| needed sounds lost | — | **0** |
| pictures that are right (precision) | 28% | **38%** |
| overall score (F1) | 0.29 | **0.40** |
| cost to a deaf viewer (lower is better) | 3.18 | **2.73** |
| pictures that start too early | 5 | **2** |

All of these are statistically solid on the 49 development clips (the 95% ranges do not cross zero).
**On those 49 clips, this is the first time the plain F1 score has improved with confidence in this
project.** The reason: an early picture was
being counted as *both* a wrong picture and a missed sound; now it counts as one correct picture.

**See it yourself:** open `data/work/timing_page/index.html`. Each sound whose picture moved has the
old video and the new video side by side and a button that plays both from just before the sound.

**One honest warning.** 3 of the 4 sounds that got fixed are the very sounds I was staring at when I
found the bug; only 1 (a glass) is a genuine surprise. So the gain here is optimistic. The real test is
the 60 TEST clips — and that would be a **second look at TEST**. That is your call (and the
supervisor's), not mine. See Actions.

## 2. "The picture leaves too early" — found the cause, not switched on yet

Pictures disappeared about 2 s before the sound stopped. The main reason is a rule that no picture
may stay longer than 8 seconds. Removing it fixes the ending (0.06 s early instead of 1.7 s) — but it
also loses one bell, because the model that checks "can the viewer already see this?" then decides it
can see the church bell. You marked that bell as *not* visible. Two reviewers said keep the 8-second
limit for now; one said remove it. **Your decision.**

## 3. Better pictures — the new version looks clearly better, but I need you to check

I looked at every picture. About a quarter were bad: two blank white squares for thunder, a
"Siren" drawn as the mythical woman, a keyboard sound drawn as one finger, an electric shaver drawn
as a man with shaving foam, a subway drawn as a steam train.

**What I tried:**
- a better instruction for *what* to draw (the object that makes the sound first; no sky; no double
  meanings);
- asking the video frames *which kind* of thing it is (it only helped once — the subway);
- removing the place from the picture (no more "crowd in front of a palace");
- a stronger picture model, **Qwen-Image**, which we already had on the cluster.

**Result, marked by me with the version hidden:** today 24 of 32 pictures readable; the new
instructions alone 25 (that is no real change — a new random seed alone moves it by that much);
**new instructions + Qwen-Image 30 of 32**, fixing 7 and breaking 1. So the gain comes from Qwen-Image,
with the instructions helping it.

The version you will rate is one step newer: the place is also removed from the picture (no palace,
no street). I looked at the four pictures that changed; all four are better, so by my eye it is at
least 30 of 32.

**Why I need you:** I wrote the new rules *and* I did the marking, so this is not independent. I also
tried to let an AI do the marking; it failed its own test (it said the "wrong" pictures were fine).

**Please rate the pictures:** open `data/work/rate_pictures/index.html`. For each picture, click Yes

Cost: Qwen-Image takes about 20 s per picture on the big GPU, instead of under 1 s.

Sound labelling — about 1 hour

Thank you! You will see 30 short video clips. For each clip there is a list of sounds someone else heard in it. Please answer the questions for each sound. Your answers are saved in this browser as you go; at the end press Export and send the file back.

The questions (use headphones; watch the whole clip first):
  • Heard? Do you hear this sound in the clip? If no, skip the rest for it.
  • Start / end: pause the video where the sound starts and press “now”; same for the end.
  • Visible? Is the thing making the sound seen making it on screen while it sounds? A barking dog on screen = yes. A baby held quietly = not visibly crying. An alarm box on a wall = not visibly ringing.
  • Obvious? With the sound off, would a viewer still know this sound is happening now? A dog on screen with its mouth open barking = yes. A phone ringing in a pocket, a siren behind the camera = no.
  • Importance: how much would you lose if you did not know this sound happened (as if the screen were black)? 1 = would not care (traffic hum, rain, wind, background birds). 2 = helps me understand the scene (footsteps, a door closing off screen). 3 = I must know it (siren, alarm, gunshot, phone, doorbell, baby). When unsure, choose the lower.
